Why \(N\) slits are better

2. Narrower peaks: Outer slits travel further → get out of phase faster → destructive interference happens sooner

Huygens' Principle
Every point on a wavefront acts as a source of secondary spherical wavelets.

The new wavefront is the envelope (tangent surface) of all these wavelets.

This explains diffraction, refraction, and why plane waves stay planar!

The Pairing Argument
Divide the slit into two halves. Pair each source in the top half with one in the bottom half, separated by \(a/2\).

Path difference for each pair: \(\dfrac{a}{2}\sin\theta\)

When \(\dfrac{a}{2}\sin\theta = \dfrac{\lambda}{2}\), each pair cancels!

First minimum: \(\boxed{a\sin\theta = \lambda}\)

Intensity Formulas
Definitions:
\(\alpha = \dfrac{2\pi d \sin\theta}{\lambda}\)   (interference)
\(\beta = \dfrac{2\pi a \sin\theta}{\lambda}\)   (diffraction)

2-Slit:
\(I = 4I_0 \cos^2\!\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}\right)\)

N-Slit (Grating):
\(I = I_0 \left[\frac{\sin(N\alpha/2)}{\sin(\alpha/2)}\right]^2\)

Single Slit:
\(I = I_0 \,\text{sinc}^2\!\left(\frac{\beta}{2}\right)\)
